Errors are |d log10| of the predicted against the Grackle value over the 25,000 steps: `T med` and `T p95` are the median and 95th percentile over time, `final` is at 2.5 Myr; every column is the median over the trajectories of the group. `cliff err` is the error of the cliff time, defined as the first row with |d ln T| > 0.05 (definition of 2026-11-16; only trajectories with a cliff; `cliffs missed` = no row above 0.05 in the prediction / trajectories with a cliff). `mass viol.` = max |ln(nH_pred / nH_0)|. `E up frac.` = fraction of steps where the specific energy increases (truth: 0).
